JohnnyJavelins

One man. Infinite javelins. Zero chill.

How to Play

  • Move with WASD / arrow keys (or drag anywhere on touch screens).
  • Johnny throws javelins automatically at the nearest monster. Prefer to aim yourself? Switch to mouse aim in Options.
  • Collect XP gems to level up and pick an upgrade. Stack them for increasingly absurd javelin chaos.
  • Max out the right pairs of upgrades to unlock Legendary evolutions.
  • Elite monsters (gold glow) and bosses drop treasure chests full of free upgrades.
  • Survive 15 minutes and slay the Ancient Dragon to win.
  • Gold you collect is kept even if you die. Spend it in The Armory on permanent upgrades and new javelin techniques.
  • Esc/P pause · 1-4 pick upgrade · R reroll
